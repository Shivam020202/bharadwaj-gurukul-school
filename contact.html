School Location Context

A Serene and Open Neighborhood

Bhardwaj Gurukul is located off State Highway 55 (connecting Begusarai and Rosera), in a clean, congestion-free environment.

The school sits in an open administrative neighborhood. Sprawling premises of the Police Line, the ITI, and residences of key officials like the District Magistrate, the S.P., and the District Judge are nearby.
