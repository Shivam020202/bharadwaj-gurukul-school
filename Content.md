## 6. About — Residential System of Education

Bhardwaj Gurukul — a commitment toward excellence in the residential system of education.

Bhardwaj Gurukul is located in a naturally serene atmosphere and pleasant environment near Bazaar Samiti, Panhas, Begusarai District, Bihar, India. The campus boasts state-of-the-art infrastructure and provides the perfect ambience for concentrated study.

The environment created for students forms a strong foundation for a better future.

Boarders are provided spacious, well-ventilated dormitories, with all basic requirements taken care of from dawn to dusk.

Health and hygiene are given top priority. Balanced, nutritious, and sumptuous food is served, with a special menu on off days. Boarders receive special coaching in all subjects, with extra attention given to below-average students.

Spoken English and grammar are part of daily classes to develop communication skills. Students with an interest in sports have ample opportunity to participate in various sporting activities.

Meditation, regular exercise, games and sports, and watching TV are part of the boarders' specially designed daily schedule — keeping students fit, mentally and physically.

## 9. Instructions to Parents — Students Leave Rules

Parents should ensure that their ward/wards return to school on the due dates after vacation/holidays. Leave applications for extension of vacation/holidays will not be entertained. If a student defaults or is absent for fifteen working days continuously without prior sanction, their name will be struck off the rolls without intimation.

### Removal of Students

Students may be removed from the rolls on any of the following grounds:
- (a) Lack of discipline
- (b) Overaged students
- (c) Default of fees
- (d) Irregular attendance and long absence

## 10. Discipline Rules

1. Students must live and let live on campus, cultivating "Fatherhood of God and Brotherhood of Man."
2. "Help ever and hurt never" must be translated into life.
3. Students are not permitted to leave the campus without prior permission.
4. Habitual neglect of studies, improper behaviour, immoral conduct, disrespect towards teachers, poor behaviour with fellow students, and irregular attendance will be sternly dealt with.
5. Students should show due regard to schoolmates and obedience to teachers.
6. Use of cigarettes, tobacco, drugs, and alcohol attracts severe punishment.
7. Stealing and willful damage of property belonging to other students, teachers, or the school will result in immediate dismissal.
8. Students are not allowed to bring or keep eatables (including pickles, ghee, medicines, tonics) or cash/valuables such as walkmans, tape recorders, mobile phones, cameras, etc. Such items will be confiscated and a fine of Rs. 250/- levied on the student concerned.
9. Students should strictly obey all rules and regulations for classrooms, hostels, dining, and the campus in general.
10. Students must converse in English at all times on campus.
11. "Courtesy begets courtesy" — use the words "Please" and "Thank you" in conversation.
12. Students should have a high sense of responsibility in their studies.
13. Students are not allowed to keep a beard or moustache on campus.

## 11. Request to Parents

1. Please keep in touch with the Director through correspondence or occasional visits, and cooperate to ensure the overall progress of your ward.
2. Parents are requested not to speak ill of the institution or staff in front of their child — this can adversely affect the child's future.
3. A disciplined child at home will be a disciplined child at school. Please do not harass your child at home; instead, encourage and instill confidence in them.
4. Please do not give or send money directly to your child.
5. Students should be brought back to school before 5:00 PM on the day previous to reopening.
6. The institution will not be responsible if a student leaves campus without the permission of the authorities.
7. While your child is at home on vacation, encourage them to continue good practices acquired from school — getting up early, regular study, helping at home, etc.
8. On arrival, record your name, address, and purpose in the visitors' register, and wait at the parents' corner. To meet any child other than your own ward, obtain permission from the Director. Entry into the hostel/dormitory is not allowed for visiting parents. On working days, parents may not meet their wards.
9. All parents are requested to cooperate with the administration and staff in complying with these directions, for the smooth functioning of the institution and to help make their children worthy citizens of India.
