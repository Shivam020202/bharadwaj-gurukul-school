About Bhardwaj Gurukul

Nurturing values, character, and academic excellence in a modern residential environment since 2005.

Our Educational Model

Residential System of Education

Bhardwaj Gurukul stands as a commitment toward excellence in the residential system of education, nestled in a naturally serene atmosphere near Bazaar Samiti, Panhas, Begusarai.

Boarders are provided spacious, well-ventilated dormitories where all basic requirements are taken care of from dawn to dusk. We prioritize health and hygiene, serving balanced, nutritious, and sumptuous meals daily.

To build physical and mental fitness, meditation, regular exercise, games, sports, and designated TV hours are integrated into the specially designed daily schedule. Spoken English and grammar are emphasizing daily classes.

Rules & Regulations

School Guidelines & Code of Conduct

Essential rules for residential and day-boarding students to ensure a safe, structured, and disciplined learning environment.

Student Discipline & Conduct

  1. Students must live and let live on campus, cultivating "Fatherhood of God and Brotherhood of Man."
  2. "Help ever and hurt never" must be translated into life.
  3. Students are not permitted to leave the campus without prior permission.
  4. Habitual neglect of studies, improper behaviour, immoral conduct, disrespect towards teachers, and irregular attendance will be sternly dealt with.
  5. Students should show due regard to schoolmates and obedience to teachers.
  6. Use of cigarettes, tobacco, drugs, and alcohol attracts severe punishment.
  7. Stealing and willful damage of property belonging to other students, teachers, or the school will result in immediate dismissal.
  8. Students are not allowed to bring or keep eatables (including pickles, ghee, medicines, tonics) or cash/valuables such as walkmans, mobile phones, cameras, etc. Such items will be confiscated and a fine of Rs. 250/- levied.
  9. Students should strictly obey all rules and regulations for classrooms, hostels, dining, and the campus.
  10. Students must converse in English at all times on campus.
  11. "Courtesy begets courtesy" — use the words "Please" and "Thank you" in conversation.
  12. Students should have a high sense of responsibility in their studies.
  13. Double check that students are not allowed to keep a beard or moustache on campus.

Cooperation Requests for Parents

  • Please keep in touch with the Director through correspondence or occasional visits, and cooperate to ensure the overall progress of your ward.
  • Parents are requested not to speak ill of the institution or staff in front of their child — this can adversely affect the child's future.
  • A disciplined child at home will be a disciplined child at school. Please do not harass your child at home; instead, encourage and instill confidence.
  • Please do not give or send money directly to your child.
  • Students should be brought back to school before 5:00 PM on the day previous to reopening.
  • The institution will not be responsible if a student leaves campus without the permission of the authorities.
  • While your child is at home on vacation, encourage them to continue good practices acquired from school — getting up early, regular study, helping at home.
  • On arrival, record details in the visitors' register, and wait at the parents' corner. Obtaining permission from the Director is required to meet any child other than your own. Entry into hostels is strictly prohibited. On working days, parents may not meet their wards.
  • All parents are requested to cooperate with the administration for the smooth functioning of the institution.

Leave Rules

Parents should ensure that their ward returns to school on the due dates after vacation/holidays. Leave applications for extension will not be entertained. If a student is absent for 15 working days continuously without prior sanction, their name will be struck off the rolls without intimation.

Grounds for Removal

  • Lack of discipline
  • Overaged students
  • Default of fees
  • Irregular attendance and long absence
