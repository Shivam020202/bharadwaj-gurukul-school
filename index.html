About the school

A residential system rooted in values

Bhardwaj Gurukul is located in a naturally serene atmosphere near Bazaar Samiti, Panhas, Begusarai. The campus provides the perfect ambience for focused study, with state-of-the-art infrastructure spread across 1,50,000 sq. ft. of green, open space.

We believe that education with a value system has no boundary. Inculcating Sanskar is more important than just marks — a principle inspired by the ancient Gurukuls and personally overseen by our Founder Chairman.

"Making the dream of Tagore come true, we created a garden by plantation across 1,50,000 square feet and run the school in a green, healthy environment. All students are like God for us — especially our female students."

— Mr. Jawahar Lal Bhardwaj, Founder Chairman

Co-Founder Director: Mr. Shiv Prakash Bhardwaj
