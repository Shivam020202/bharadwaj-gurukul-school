Our Facilities & Hostel Life

Discover state-of-the-art facilities, structured academic hours, a highly nutritious weekly menu, and campus boarding checklists.

Everything Under One Roof

Campus Facilities & Highlights

A green and healthy environment across 1,50,000 square feet, providing children the perfect ambience for concentrated study.

Separate Hostels

Separate, well-equipped, and spacious hostels for boys and girls with dawn-to-dusk basic requirements fully handled.

5 Nutritious Meals

Sumptuous vegetarian food served 5 times a day, containing milk, curd, ghee, fruit, sweets, and ice cream on special days.

Computer & Audio-Visual Lab

Modern computer systems along with large screens for screening educational or motivational films every Sunday.

Sports & Multi-purpose Hall

A large playground and a 5,100 sq. ft. multi-purpose hall with equipment for cricket, volleyball, taekwondo, badminton, chess, etc.

Purified Chilled Water

Multiple water coolers with advanced purification systems serving hygienic, cold drinking water campus-wide.

Tours & Transport

Dedicated school transport bus for historical tours and regular local visits to tourist sites in Bihar for growth.

Laundry & Grooming

Regular laundry/washing service and on-campus salon facility to maintain boarders' optimal hygiene standards.

Quiet Library

Rich repository of school textbooks, references, reading materials, and resources to promote learning habits.

24-Hr Power & Guards

Uninterrupted 24-hour electricity supply and dedicated security guards maintaining absolute campus safety.

Unique Highlights

  • Classes 1–3: English and Maths taught jointly by two teachers in a fixed timetable.
  • School fees and hostel charges are highly affordable while offering 3 more hours of daily study.
  • Visits by eminent musicians, artists, and national personalities performing live 3–4 times a year.
  • Semi-annual school suggestions/feedbacks and parent-teacher-student dialogue twice a year.
